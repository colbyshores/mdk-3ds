2026-11-17  level 7  texture tile order is real on the GPU and is what batch 173 was seeing
============================================================================================

CLAIM. `mdk_3ds/src/render/c3d/04_textures.inc` expands every texture into a plain
CPU buffer and hands it to `C3D_TexLoadImage`, which `memcpy`s it into the
texture's linear buffer without swizzling. The PICA200 therefore reads that
buffer in 8x8 Morton tile order, so writing it row-major permutes every texel
within its tile. Mutating `pica::textureTexelIndex` back to row-major changes the
SAMPLED PIXELS on the emulator by roughly doubling their gradient energy, and
the same mutation reproduces the pre-fix capture. The tiled build samples
smoothly. This is the GPU-side UV->texel rule the batch-173 row asked for, stated
as an addressing fact rather than a scale factor.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED. This does not close batch 173's HUD-panel period (5.3 vs
21.3 crop px) by itself, does not re-open the `/4` UV idea (still forbidden:
it broke the health digits), and is not hardware evidence. The hardware
acceptance rows stay open.

SCENE. Level 7, harness auto-boot (`screen=4 level=7`), player feet (0,4,0),
yaw 90, health 100, ~4260 frames elapsed, mono, software renderer.

`|dx|`/`|dy|` are the mean absolute luminance gradients inside the non-clear
mask of the crop. THE TILED BUILD HAS HALF THE HIGH-FREQUENCY ENERGY of the
row-major build in the same scene. That is what plain `memcpy` of row-major data
into a Morton-tiled texture should look like: every 8x8 tile is transposed
within itself, so adjacent screen pixels jump between unrelated texels and the
gradient energy roughly doubles.

CLEAR-COLOUR COVERAGE IS THE WRONG METRIC HERE, and that is worth writing down
because it is the reason two earlier "no change" readings were not a refutation.
Scrambling texels permutes colour, it does not change alpha, and these level
textures are opaque, so no fragment is gained or lost and coverage cannot move.
The first comparisons used only coverage plus raw pixel-difference fractions
(same-build 9.4%, mutant-vs-fixed 11.3%), which are animation-noise sized in a
scene that animates per frame. The gradient-energy split is 2x and does not hide
inside that noise.

MUTATION-AS-CONTROL. `textureTexelIndex` was replaced by
`return y*paddedWidth + x;` (the pre-fix body), built, captured, and restored
byte-for-byte; the restored header rebuilds and the host suites below are re-run
against it. The pre-fix 23:03 capture landing in the mutant's column, not the
fixed column, is the check that the mutation reproduced the defect and not some
third state.

HOST EVIDENCE (the part that needs no instrument). `tests/scripts/vm_tests`
runs `testTheTexelTileOrder()`, which pins the permutation, the tile anchors,
and the recorded Morton-read-as-linear signatures (period 32 -> ~8 px,
left/right split -> horizontal bands). `tests/scripts/c3d_frame_test.sh` pins
that both expansions write through `pica::textureTexelIndex` and that the
transparent padding survives. Both are green on the restored header.
