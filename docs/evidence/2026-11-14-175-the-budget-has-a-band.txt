1. F1 (Medium) — CONFIRMED, then FIXED. THE TOLERANCE WAS THE SUBSTANCE OF THE
   CHECK AND NOTHING PINNED IT.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reviewer's claim: every positional arm of the reach check is pinned in its LOGIC
but its TOLERANCE is pinned only between 2 and 88 crop px, because every ink fixture
is anchored to `HUD_INK_REACH` and so MOVES when the budget moves. Measured by the
reviewer: `HUD_INK_REACH` = 40/60/88 → 296 PASS, rc 0.

RE-MEASURED HERE, NOT ACCEPTED ON THE REVIEWER'S WORD. The parent test file run
against the parent harness with only that constant changed:

  parent test + parent harness, HUD_INK_REACH=88 ......... rc 0, "PASS EmurunHarness
                                                         (296 checks)"
  NEW test + the same harness at HUD_INK_REACH=88 ........ rc 1, named refusal

So the finding is exactly right, and the reviewer's own scenario is the one that
matters: the record says a budget that reddens its own boundary gets tuned rather
than deleted, so tuning upward IS the expected move, and it was free.

THE POLICY (this answers review-2 open question 1, which said the remediation was a
shape and not a number without it). A budget is a CALIBRATION: a measurement times a
margin. So the measurement became a constant and the band is derived from it:

    HUD_INK_SHORTFALL = 2     # measured on the shipped capture: bbox far corner
                              # 388,232 against the derived 390,234
    HUD_INK_REACH     = 12    # six times the measurement (unchanged from batch 174)
    HUD_INK_REACH_MAX = 20    # ten times it, EXCLUSIVE: 19 is legal, 20 is not

and `hud_ink_verdict` now refuses a budget outside [SHORTFALL, REACH_MAX):

    emurun: HUD MALFORMED: the reach budget is 88 crop px and
              that is outside its calibration band [2, 20) -- the measured texel
              shortfall is 2 px and the ceiling is ten times it. Do not
              widen the budget past the band: re-measure the shortfall on a
              capture, restate HUD_INK_SHORTFALL, and say which capture.

Three things about that are deliberate.

* IT IS IN THE SHIPPED INSTRUMENT, NOT ONLY IN THE TEST FILE. A test someone can
  choose not to run is not a gate. The test file ALSO refuses to build its fixtures
  against an out-of-band budget, because the fixtures are anchored to the budget and
  a block pushed above `HUD_INK_Y0` would red as `HUD INK NOT UNIQUE` — the
  reviewer's own REACH=89 observation, where the row reddened for a fixture reason
  and not a measurement. A row must not red that way; now it says what is wrong.
* THE CEILING IS EXCLUSIVE, so the absolute fixture at shortfall = REACH_MAX is
  refused by EVERY legal budget. That fixture (`inkband`) is the one ink fixture
  NOT anchored to the budget, and it is the row that catches a loosening.
* THE REVIEWER'S ALTERNATIVE WAS MEASURED AND DOES NOT WORK. "Derive a maximum from
  the rect (`HUD_INK_X1/4` or similar)": 390/4 = 97, which admits the 88 the finding
  is about. A rect fraction is not a calibration; a multiple of the measurement is.

THE NEW FIXTURES, and what each pins (all derived from the harness's own constants
except `inkband`, which is the point):

  inkshort        shortfall == HUD_INK_SHORTFALL (2)   -> PASSES. The budget must
                    cover the measurement it was derived from. This replaces the
                    accidental lower pin the reviewer found (REACH=0 reddened four
                    unrelated padded-count rows).
  inkreach        shortfall == HUD_INK_REACH           -> PASSES (inclusive edge).
  inkreachshort   shortfall == HUD_INK_REACH + 1       -> REFUSES by name. The
                    failing side of the edge, which nothing sat on before.
  inkband         shortfall == HUD_INK_REACH_MAX (20), ABSOLUTE -> REFUSES.
  inkreachx       X one past the budget, Y flush       -> REFUSES.
  inkreachy       Y one past the budget, X flush       -> REFUSES.
