HD layer (docs/HD_TEXTURES_3DS_SPEC.md).

DESIGN.
  - The chain of an ORIGINAL texture (lossless RGBA8) is built on the CPU at
    texture creation, inside the expansion that already owns the buffer:
    pica::buildMipChain downsamples each level 2x2 (downsampleTiled, on the
    Morton-tiled layout) and re-runs prepareForFiltering at the level's scaled
    real size, levels consecutive after level 0; makeTexture hands each level
    to C3D_TexLoadImage from its offset. Not citro3d's C3D_TexGenerateMipmap: a
    blocking display transfer per level, and a texture can be made again
    mid-frame after an eviction.
  - The HD layer's chains are NOT built here: tex3ds builds and ETC-compresses
    them on the host (-f etc1 / etc1a4 -q high -m gaussian) and the runtime
    imports them into the same slots, under the same per-draw controls. Byte
    accounting is already per format (pica::textureBytes: RGBA8 / ETC1 / ETC1A4,
    chain included) and is what makeTexture's eviction and
    mdk_render_index_texture_bytes (Kurt's sprite residency budget) use.
  - The toggle: a texture is MADE with a chain while enableMipmaps is on (its
    memory is real and measurable); the sampler's use is live -- maxLevel and
    the mip filter are written per draw (trilinear with FILTERING), and held at
    level 0 when the toggle is off (the original look exactly), for an animated
    texture (stacked frames), and for Original-shaded draws (the sky and the
    canvas, which the PC filters without a chain).
  Host-pinned: pica_matrix_tests testTheMipChain (level counts; the box filter
  exact through the tile index; a half-covered 2x2 is alpha 128; buildMipChain
  equals a downsample; per-format byte counts). Config/menu: seventh toggle and
  row (tests/formats). Frame gate: the sink's producer resolution now follows
  pointer arithmetic into a buffer it can see (`pixels + offset`, or a member
  kept on the record); arithmetic on a call's result is still refused (new arm:
  `texture.source.data() + offset`).

MEASURED.
  Linear heap free after load (mem[loop] lin=), FILTERING on, MIPMAPS off -> on:
    level 3  16,353,792 -> 14,287,104   (2.07 MB for the chains)
    level 6  18,560,256 -> 17,192,448   (1.37 MB)
  (Includes chains made for sky/canvas textures that the sampler never
  descends; the texture does not know its use when it is made -- to fix with the

  All toggles off: L7 frame bit-identical to the pre-mipmap build; L3 differs
  only in 6 pixels that ALSO differ between two runs of the same build (the
  nondeterministic object, docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md).
  Filtering + mipmaps vs filtering only, level 6 (left filtered, middle +mips,
  right PC enhanced): docs/evidence/2026-11-17-3ds-mipmaps-L6.png. The distant
  floor is much softer than the PC's, which pairs its chain with 16x anisotropy
  the PICA does not have; a negative LOD bias trades that blur for shimmer and
  is a value to set on hardware, not here.

REVIEW (independent, same day; citro3d's texture.o disassembled to confirm the
consecutive level layout, the per-level upload size, the LOD register written
from tex->maxLevel at draw time, and that C3D_TexFlush's size reads maxLevel --
reset before the flush). No blocking defect. What changed:
  F1 MEDIUM -- MIPMAPS with FILTERING off kept the oracle's > 0 cut, so a lower
     level's averaged alpha drew partial texels opaque and cut-outs thickened with
     distance. The sampler now descends a chain only while FILTERING is on too
     (the PC always pairs its chain with the half cover).
  F2 MEDIUM -- an allocation that could not fit the chain failed the TEXTURE once
     nothing was left to evict. It now retries once without the chain, logged.
  F4 -- testTheMipChain gains a padded two-level case: each level at makeTexture's
     upload offset, equal to the primitives applied level by level at the scaled
     real size, and the total equal to textureBytes.
  F9 -- stale comments (02_lifecycle.inc, render_types.h's toggle note: the PC
     always mips; the toggle is the 3DS's).
  Recorded, not changed: animated textures also get a chain they never sample
  (with sky and canvas textures, the "unsampled chain" cost above); Kurt's sprite
  cache records a frame's cost when it is made, so flipping MIPMAPS mid-level lets
  the residency budget drift until the next load; an evicted texture made again
  mid-frame now also builds its chain (an Old-3DS hitch to measure); the frame
  gate's producer scan cannot see a non-call reassignment of the buffer (an older
  limit, now also covering `pixels + offset`).
