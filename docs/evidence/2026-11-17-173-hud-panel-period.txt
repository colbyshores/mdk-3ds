2026-11-17  HUD panel  fine stripe is gone: 5.3 px -> a 2-lobe oval
==================================================================

CLAIM. Batch 173's headline symptom -- "the HUD's panel sprite reaches the panel
as a fine vertical stripe instead of the oval, at a period of ~5.3 crop px where
the derived geometry says 21.3" -- is the row-major texture layout. On the
tiled build the panel carries no 5.3 px component and resolves into two coarse
lobes across its 58 crop px, which is the oval. The row-major mutant reproduces
the recorded 5.3 px peak exactly.

CAPTURES. Both are `--expect hud-visible --shot`, mono, level 7, `screen=4`,
feet (0,4,0), yaw 90, health 100, ~4200 frames, same scene, same emulator.

  (a) row-major mutant  `2026-11-17-hud-panel-a-rowmajor.png`
  (b) Morton (shipped)  `2026-11-17-hud-panel-b-morton.png`

(440,239)-(840,479) of the 1280x1024 Xvfb frame -- enlarged 8x with
nearest-neighbour so the striping is visible in a diff. The numbers below come
from the UNSCALED crop, luminance averaged over the panel's rows y=200..237,
x=342..399, detrended with a quadratic fit:

  build      dominant period   fine-band (3-9 px) peak   stripes   profile sd
  ---------  ---------------   -----------------------   -------   ----------
  row-major       11.6 px               5.3 px              5        11.2
  Morton          29.0 px               6.4 px              2        16.3

The mutant's fine-band peak lands on 5.3 px, the exact number recorded in
docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md for this row before any tile change. The tiled build has no
peak there; its structure is 29 px across, i.e. about two features in 58 px,
against the 21.3 px the panel geometry predicts for an 84-texel sprite mapped to
56 crop px. Counting upward instead of measuring the peak: 5 stripes -> 2.
