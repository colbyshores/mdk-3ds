2026-11-17  HUD panel  fine stripe is gone: 5.3 px -> a 2-lobe oval
==================================================================

CLAIM. Batch 173's headline symptom -- "the HUD's panel sprite reaches the panel
as a fine vertical stripe instead of the oval, at a period of ~5.3 crop px where
the derived geometry says 21.3" -- is the row-major texture layout. On the
tiled build the panel carries no 5.3 px component and resolves into two coarse
lobes across its 58 crop px, which is the oval. The row-major mutant reproduces
the recorded 5.3 px peak exactly.
