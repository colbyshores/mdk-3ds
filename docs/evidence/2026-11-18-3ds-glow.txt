3DS Enhanced Mode glow (enhancement 5 of 7, SPEC.md 7.9) -- EMULATOR evidence
==============================================================================

Instrument: Mandarine r1.2.1 (20250128-418c25c, md5 0d2adab6...), via
mdk_3ds/tools/compare_linux.sh -> emurun.sh, DISPLAY=:99, forced llvmpipe OpenGL.
Builds: make -C mdk_3ds AUTOQUIT=90 FIXED=1 CAPTURE=300 LEVEL=<n>. Fixed 1/60 s.
NOT a hardware result (the appliance is pinned to another project; the owner
tests on hardware later, over FTP).

WHAT IT IS
  The PC's post.frag shine, on the PICA200, behind enableGlowBillboards (the
  existing GLOW row), when the enhanced rig is on:
      blurred = to_linear((scene@mip3 + scene@mip5) / 2)
      shine   = clamp(max(blurred - 0.8, 0) * 0.8)
      colour  = 1 - (1 - colour)(1 - shine)
  Per eye, between the scene and the canvas (06_draw.inc drawGlow), all GPU:
    1. C3D_FrameSplit (the eye's scene is submitted first);
    2. at edge-smoothing scale 2, a 2x2-averaging transfer to 240x400;
    3. GX_TextureCopy (a raw copy), 50 tile rows of 7680 bytes, into a 256x512
       RGBA8 VRAM texture;
    4. blur levels 1-5 as small separate textures (128x256 .. 8x16), each a
       render-to-texture quad sampling the level above at texel corners (an
       exact 2x2 average), each followed by a framebuffer flush in the command
       list;
    5. a 32x64 texture := level 3, then += level 5 at constant alpha 1/2: the
       PC's (mip3 + mip5) / 2 in ONE texture;
    6. one full-panel quad into the eye: a six-stage combiner (the threshold and
       the fitted curve below) and a screen blend.
  One texture unit (0). VRAM ~690 KB, released and remade whenever the eye
  targets are rebuilt. GX queue: +2 entries per eye (+1 at scale 2), of 32.

THE APPROXIMATION (pica_matrix.h, THE GLOW)
  The PICA has no power function and blends sRGB values. The threshold is
  0.9036 sRGB (0.8 linear). No blend factor reproduces a linear-light screen on
  every background, so the shine screened is the curve that minimises the
  squared error against the PC's result over all backgrounds, drawn as
  P*min(1, u/u0) + Q*u (within 2.8/255 of that curve). Modelled against the
  PC's formula: worst case 30/255 (the PC's own linear shine screened in sRGB
  would miss by 69/255). Host-tested (pica_matrix_tests.cpp): the constants are
  reachable by the combiner, both quad mappings, both blend states.

HOW IT GOT HERE (each step measured; probes were temporary builds)
  - First mapping mirrored and offset by the 112 padding rows: t runs down from
    memory row 0. Fixed (2026-11-18-3ds-glow-blur-aligned.png).
  - A first version sampled level 5 on texture unit 1. Unit 1 samples
    TEXCOORD1 (flat grey under the scene shader), and -- from the review of the
    linked libcitro3d.a -- C3D_TexBind(1, NULL) is a null dereference on the
    console and a bound unit cannot be unbound. Now unit 0 only.
  - The same review: C3D_TexGenerateMipmap is a CPU box filter for a linear
    texture (no GX call in its body), so it was a CPU path, and it read the
    previous frame's copy. Now the transfer engine and render-to-texture draws.
  - citro3d renders only into VRAM (C3D_RenderTargetCreateFromTex refuses
    anything else: the linear-heap texture's targets came back null).
  - Rendering into the big texture's own mip levels never reached the sampler
    in the emulator (level 4 a flat texel, level 5 black, the mix left level 3
    unchanged); separate single-level textures do.
  - Second review: the transfer engine's tiled-to-tiled scaling is a path
    citro3d itself never uses, so the console's behaviour at these widths is
    unproven -- the blur levels are now render-to-texture too (only the scale-2
    shrink still uses it: a hardware check). A per-pass C3D_FrameSplit would
    have used ~28 of the GX queue's 32 entries in stereo at scale 2 (a full
    queue is svcBreak): the passes flush inside the command list instead. Glow
    VRAM allocated before edge smoothing could fragment the eye targets' space:
    it is released and remade around every eye-target rebuild.
  The source pins the two library traps (c3d_frame_test.sh
  no-cpu-mipmaps-and-no-texture-units-above-0, with an arm for each), the
  split before the copy and the flush after every blur pass, the glow's place
  in the replay, and the state it restores (11 arms in all).

MEASUREMENTS (level 3, frame 300)
  Against an exact numerical model of this pipeline (box levels, the mix,
  bilinear sampling, byte-quantised combiner constants, screen blend) run on
  the glow-off capture, Kurt's known nondeterministic patch excluded: measured
  lift total 790 vs model 765 (in /255 units), mean |measured - model| on lifted
  pixels 0.68/255, correlation of the shine 0.999; pixels lifted >4: 21 vs 16;
  >16: 4 vs 4. The final all-render-to-texture version differs from the
  transfer-engine version in 7 pixels, all inside that patch.
  Visible effect is small in this scene, as on the PC: the shine falls on sky
  that is already near white, where a screen adds almost nothing.
  Levels 7 and 8 at frame 300: nothing above the threshold (the PC's threshold,
  by design). The edge-smoothing (scale 2) and stereo runs are in the commit's
  verification.

NOT VERIFIED (the hardware pass)
  - Cost per eye on the Old 3DS: a 384 KB copy, seven small render-to-texture
    passes (the largest 128x256), one full-panel quad (plus a 1.5 MB averaging
    transfer at scale 2). The owner A/Bs the toggle.
  - The scale-2 shrink's tiled-to-tiled scaled transfer (see the review note).
  - GX/render-to-texture ordering and cache behaviour on real hardware.
  - A scene with a strong glow source over a dark surface: none of the fixed
    harness frames has one; the arithmetic is verified against the model.
