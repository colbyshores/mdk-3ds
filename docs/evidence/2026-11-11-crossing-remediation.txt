2. THE NEW LEVEL-7 DISCRIMINATOR (review-1 F3)
----------------------------------------------
The old L7 golden had one line, `walk_655_to_687 current=DANT_2`, and the reviewer
showed a geometric-arena tick passes it: `ArenaSpace::arenaAt(0, y, -3)` answers
CDANT_1 up to y = 675 and DANT_2 from y = 676, and the walk ended at y = 687, where
both rules agree. Measured again here, on the built core, after the step to y = 675:

    L7 s=675 current=DANT_2 geometry=CDANT_1

`current` is the crossing rule (connection 1001, plane y = 673, the C#'s `to.Y >
min.Y` is strict, so the step to 674 crosses and 675 is one step inside);
`geometry` is `ArenaSpace::arenaAt` under the feet, which the oracle does NOT use
for CurrentArena. Printing the pair is what makes the line a discriminator.

Provenance, stated honestly: the C# does not assert the arena at y = 675 — it
asserts only at the end of its own walk loop (`DoorArenaTests.cs:76-79` is the
loop, `:81` the assertion). The value is the C#'s crossing predicate applied to
the shipped DTI, and it was predicted from that rule (and independently by the
reviewer's own probe) BEFORE this build printed it. It is not a new oracle
assertion and the golden's header says so.

4. THE EFFECT GATE ON FollowArenas (review-1 F6)
------------------------------------------------

`ApplyVelocity` (`:98`) and therefore before `FollowArenas` (`:106`). The port's
call site had no such gate, and `previous_position` is written only for a

call is now `!obj->dead && obj->effect_frames == 0 && (flags & FlagChangesArena)`.
Latent today: `effect_frames` has no writer in the port yet, so no shipped
behaviour moves — which is why no golden changes.
