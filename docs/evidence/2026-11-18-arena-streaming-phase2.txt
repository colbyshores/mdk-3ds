1. WHAT CHANGED
---------------
- formats::Texture pixels can be dropped and read back; the object (name, size,
  frames, ADDRESS) stays. A reader of away pixels asks the arena's pixel source.
- mdk::ArenaStream (LevelData::stream): the original's arena_switch /
  arena_load_step / BSPShow for an arena's texture section (60-72% of the MTO).
  One MTO arena's pixels resident; a switch drops the old arena BEFORE reading
  the new; 32 KiB per frame; a DRAWN arena is finished at once (a logged stall);
  anything the frame draws keeps its pixels whatever the scripts' pair says.
  Geometry, BSP and models stay resident for every arena, so the simulation
  cannot depend on stream progress.
- ScriptRuntime::updateDrawnArenas writes obj.visible (C# UpdateArenas
  :696-699): objects of undrawn arenas are no longer drawn.
- mdk::ArenaViews: a LevelView per kept arena (loaded pair, drawn list, the
  stream's resident arena and its connected corridors), built on entry, freed
  with its palette's GPU textures on exit; failed builds retried; orphaned
  palettes swept. Both frontends: scripts -> stream.update -> views.sync ->
  draw(drawn list). Before this, ONE view (the start arena) was ever drawn.
- Harness: `make AT=x,y,z,yaw` (3DS), MDK_COMPARE_AT, MDK_FRAME_AT /
  MDK_FRAME_WAIT / MDK_FRAME_TRUE_ASPECT.

----------------------------------------------------
Level 3's corridor CHMO_1 carries the designed sequence in its DTI records:
x=82 a type-3 record PRELOADS HMO_2, x=147 a type-1 record SHOWS it.
  preload (AT=85,700,110):  "stream: switch to HMO_2 (704180 bytes ...)" ->
    22 steps, one per frame -> "stream: HMO_2 in"; no stall; "views: 1 built
    (last HMO_2, 41.4 ms), 1 released" (HMO_1's).
  show at once (AT=150,...): "stream: STALL HMO_2 shown before its stream was
    done: 704180 bytes in 266.5 ms" -- a warp into the show box, the original's
    blocking BSPShow. Walking, the 65 units between the two records are the
    time the stream has; whether they suffice on the console's SD is a hardware
    number.

6. INDEPENDENT REVIEW (Fable subagent, code-only) -- no blocker; fixed from it
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- the stream and views followed the tick's END (current/second) while the frame
  draws the tick's MIDDLE (drawnArenas): a scripted teleport would have drawn no
  world for a frame and demand-loaded the arena just left. One drawn list now
  feeds stream, views and draw;
- textures made through a palette whose arena has no view were never released
  (effects/debris left behind): the orphan sweep;
- a switch freed the old arena last (peak old + staging + new): it now trims
  first, and again before any read when something stops being kept;
- a released 3DS texture kept its heap capacity (clear -> swap);
- a transient mesh allocation failure hid an arena for the level: retried;
- each 32 KiB step re-resolved the path (directory listings copied): resolved
  once per Mto;
- partial adoption could leave a texture "away" for ever; unmanaged arenas were
  dropped at load.
