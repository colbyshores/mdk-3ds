Doorway-crossing evidence — batch 134 (ledger row 3ds.collision-doors)
=====================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
The measured artefact behind golden/L4_crossing.txt and golden/L7_crossing.txt,
the ported FindConnections / CrossedArena / CrossesDoorway / FollowArenas in
mdk_core/src/scripts/script_runtime.cpp, and the `make -C tests/scripts
test_crossing` target.

PROVENANCE OF THE EXPECTED VALUES — read this first.
No C# was executed for this batch. The .NET SDK this loop used for oracle probes
(/tmp/kilo/dotnet10, 10.0.402) is not on this host any more: /tmp/kilo is empty
and `dotnet` is not on PATH (checked: `which dotnet`, `find / -maxdepth 4 -name
dotnet -type f` -> nothing). So every expected value in the two goldens is
transcribed from the ORACLE'S OWN committed test suite, which is read-only in
this repository:

  tests/Mdk.Game.Tests/ArenaSwitchTests.cs   (level 4, MEAT_7 <-> CMEAT_7)
  tests/Mdk.Game.Tests/DoorArenaTests.cs     (level 7, CDANT_1 -> DANT_2)

Those tests were written against the original game ("Found by playtest", in
their own words) and they are the oracle's recorded assertions, not this
implementation's output. Each golden line cites the assertion it comes from.
Fields no oracle test asserts are NOT in the golden.

1. THE CONNECTION SURVEY (levels 3-8, from the shipped DTIs)
-----------------------------------------------------------
    L3 arenas=19 type6=28 ids=14 ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=0 1 2 3 6 7
    L4 arenas=19 type6=28 ids=14 ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=0 1 2 3
    L5 arenas=19 type6=16 ids=8  ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=6 7
    L6 arenas=19 type6=32 ids=16 ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=0 1 2 3 6 7
    L7 arenas=19 type6=32 ids=16 ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=0 1 2 3
    L8 arenas=19 type6=32 ids=16 ids_not_pairing_2=0 dirs=0 1 2 3 6 7
    ALL_DIRS: 0 1 2 3 6 7

  * `dirs` is the DTI record's angle field reinterpreted as the C#'s Doorway
    enum: 0 -X, 1 +X, 2 -Y, 3 +Y, 4 Left, 5 Right, 6 -Z, 7 +Z.
  * Every connection id pairs EXACTLY TWO arenas in every shipped level
    (ids_not_pairing_2 = 0), so the C#'s "last of a group of three wins"
    tie-break in FindConnections is unreachable on this data. It is ported
    anyway, because the C# is the oracle, and it is what a future level would
    need.
  * 4 and 5 (the diagonals) appear in NO shipped level, which is what the C#'s
    comment ("unused by the levels") claims, and it is now measured.
  * 6 and 7 (the hatches) appear in levels 3, 5, 6 and 8, and level 5's
    doorways are ONLY hatches. See section 4 — this is the one that was nearly
    lost.

2. THE TWO PINNED DOORWAYS
--------------------------
Level 4, the oracle's ArenaSwitchTests case (connection 1012):

    CONN arena=MEAT_7  id=1012 dir=3 pos=-6.00,14822.00,21.00 box=6.00,14822.00,33.00
    CONN arena=CMEAT_7 id=1012 dir=2 pos=-6.00,14822.00,21.00 box=6.00,14822.00,33.00

  dir 3 = +Y on the room's side, dir 2 = -Y on the corridor's side: the same
  plane y = 14822 seen from each arena, which is what makes the crossing
  reversible. The geometric arena at the test's points (ArenaSpace::arenaAt,
  reachable arenas only):

    arenaAt(0,14722,21) = MEAT_7      <- where the oracle teleports Kurt
    arenaAt(0,14821,21) = CMEAT_7     <- INSIDE THE CORRIDOR'S BOX, not through
                                        the doorway: the bug the test is named
                                        for
    arenaAt(0,14825,21) = CMEAT_7

  So the first tick's geometric answer and the oracle's teleport agree at the
  start point, and at y = 14821 they deliberately do not — which is the whole
  content of the golden's `s=2 current=MEAT_7`.

Level 7, the oracle's DoorArenaTests case (connection 1001):

    CONN arena=DANT_2  id=1001 dir=2 pos=-13.00,673.00,-3.00 box=13.00,673.00,18.00
    CONN arena=CDANT_1 id=1001 dir=3 pos=-13.00,673.00,-3.00 box=13.00,673.00,18.00

    arenaAt(0,655,-3) = CDANT_1       <- where the oracle teleports Kurt
    arenaAt(0,688,-3) = DANT_2

  The walk is the test's own loop (one tick per unit of y, 655..687). The
  crossing happens on the step to y = 674, because the C#'s predicate is
  `to.Y > min.Y` (strict) and `from.Y <= min.Y`: from 673 to 674 is the first
  move that satisfies both.

3. WHAT THE GOLDENS PIN, AND THE MUTATIONS THAT SHOW IT
-------------------------------------------------------
`make -C tests/scripts test_crossing` -> rc 0, both cases PASS.

  M1 — the pre-batch state (the tick never consults CrossedArena):
       FAIL L4_crossing: L4 s=3 current=MEAT_7 (expected CMEAT_7)
       FAIL L7_crossing: L7 walk_655_to_687 current=CDANT_1 (expected DANT_2)
       -> rc 1. Both cases discriminate; the frozen arena is caught on both
       levels.
  M2 — the +Y boundary made strict (`from.y <= minY` -> `from.y < minY`):
       FAIL L7_crossing (current=CDANT_1), L4 still PASS.
       -> the "a move that starts exactly on the plane comes from the far side"
       half is pinned, by level 7 only.
  M3 — DoorwayDrop 5 -> 0: BOTH CASES STILL PASS.
       -> NOT PINNED. Both pinned doorways have their record's z at or above
       Kurt's feet, so the 5-unit skirt below a horizontal doorway is not what
       decides either case. Recorded as unpinned rather than dressed up.
  M4 — the two hatch arms removed (see section 4): both goldens still PASS.
       -> NOT PINNED either. Stated plainly below.

4. THE HATCH ARMS: LOAD-BEARING, AND NOT PINNED
-----------------------------------------------
A synthetic move straight down through each dir-6 record's own plane, on the
shipped data, with the arms ported:

    L3 arena=HMO_2    id=1002 dir=6 pos=-21.00,1092.00,68.00  => CHMO_2
    L3 arena=HMO_8    id=1009 dir=6 pos=378.00,2419.00,-263.00 => CHMO_6
    L5 arena=MUSE_1   id=1000 dir=6 pos=-12.00,-10.00,-3.00   => CMUSE_1
    L5 arena=MUSE_2   id=1002 dir=6 pos=197.00,86.00,-580.00  => CMUSE_2
    L6 arena=OLYM_3   id=1004 dir=6 pos=-1703.00,193.00,-680.00 => COLYM_3
    L8 arena=GUNT_4   id=1007 dir=6 pos=-431.00,1430.00,-192.00 => CGUNT_4
    (and the dir-7 side of each pair correctly answers "-" for the same
    downward move: a hatch opens one way per record.)

With the two arms deleted, EVERY one of those answers becomes "-" — the hatches
are sealed, and on level 5 that is every doorway in the level. The goldens stay
green either way, so this is a real gap in the pin, not a hypothetical one.

WHY IT IS NOT PINNED: the oracle's test suite has no hatch-crossing assertion
(ArenaSwitchTests is level 4's +Y door; DoorArenaTests is level 7's;
AirlockTests asserts door states, not CurrentArena), and no oracle run was
possible on this host. Inventing an expected value here would be the
implementation grading itself, which is the one thing a golden may not do. The
arms are ported from the C#'s text and demonstrated on the shipped geometry;
the pin needs either a dotnet probe or a new oracle assertion, and it is
recorded as open.

5. WHAT ELSE THIS DOES NOT EVIDENCE
-----------------------------------
  * FollowArenas (the per-object FlagChangesArena crossing) is ported and wired
    at the C#'s call site, but no golden exercises it: it needs a
    FlagChangesArena object that actually moves across a doorway, and the oracle
    test that would pin it (DoorArenaTests' door.Arena assertions) also needs
    PreloadArena / ShowArena("") / the drawn-arena list, none of which is
    ported.
  * TeleportKurt is not ported, so "an arena no doorway leads to" is
    unreachable; the first tick's geometric arena stands in for the oracle's
    teleport, and section 2 shows where the two agree.
  * The frontends still pin the collision world from the geometric arena under
    the feet (arena_space.h's setSolid note), because the oracle passes the LIST
    of current + active second and ArenaSpace::setSolid takes one name. Making
    currentArena_ the frontend's pin is the next batch, and it needs a two-name
    setSolid first.
  * Spans' inclusivity at the doorway's x/z edges is not pinned (both pinned
    crossings sit strictly inside).
  * Emulator evidence for this batch is a level-7 idle run (Kurt does not move),
    so it shows no regression and does not exercise a single crossing.
