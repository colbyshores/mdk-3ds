1. WHAT THE ORIGINAL DOES (GHIDRA, rechecked against the binary)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- LEVELnO.SNI is never resident. Its DIRECTORY is (0x57f378, 24-byte entries,
  count 0x57f374) and the file stays open (handle 0x491b44).
- ONE music buffer (0x491b40). 0x431590 looks the arena's two music names up in
  that directory and refuses more than 0x7d000 = 512,000 bytes for the pair
  ("Sounds %s %s overflow buffersize"). The names are the two pascal strings of
  the CMI's arena record (0x43d9cc); the retail first one is NONE.
- arena_load starts the preload (0x4192c4 -> 0x431590, flag 0x573b2c = 1);
  game_frame reads a step a frame (0x4193a4 -> 0x4317d8): 0xf768 bytes while
  more than 0x10000 remain, then the rest. When the last step lands and the
  arena is Kurt's, the track starts (0x419158, the fades).
- A corridor is read from the same file when it is needed (0x431914: seek to
  the entry, a blocking read loop in the same step size, parse).
- THE 24-BYTE SOUND ENTRIES OF AN MTO ARENA'S MODELS SECTION (spec section 4):
  name[12], u16 flags, u16, u32 offset (from the models section), u32 length --
  the SNI entry's own layout. arena_load_step (0x41983c), on the stream's last
  chunk, registers them from the streamed buffer (0x4310cc -> 0x403a7c(flags,
  models + offset)) into the 16 handles at 0x573b94; more than 16 is fatal. They
  are the arena's OWN effects, they live in the arena's block, and they go when
  the arena goes.

2. WHAT THE FILES SAY
---------------------
  level  LEVELnO.SNI   music tracks (bytes)   corridors (bytes)   S + TRAVERSE
    3    3,484,332     7  (3,290,490)         9  (193,395)        905,728
    8    4,988,896     9  (4,482,651)         9  (505,754)        832,536
Every track is mono PCM, 8- or 16-bit, 465-512 KB. CORRIDOR (30 KB) is in
LEVELnS.SNI. A SPRITE ANIMATION's directory length is 0xFFFFFFFF in all six
retail cases (LEVEL4S.SNI K_SURF, K_SURFJ; LEVEL6S.SNI K_SLIP, K_SLIDE,
K_BSLIDE, K_FSLIDE): its size is the u32 its entry starts with, which does not
count itself.
The arenas' embedded sounds: 12-16 names a level (level 3: BUBBLE1, DBOARD,
DOORLAND, H9CRASH, H9IDLE, H9START, MARIO, SMASH, SPLAT, ...), 280-944 KB a
level, always the contiguous TAIL of the models section, and NOT ONE of the 85
names is in any SNI archive. The C# oracle parses them (Arena.Sounds) and
nothing reads them, so neither the oracle nor this port plays them: see
docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md. Level 3's HMO_2 names AMB_DRIP as its music and HMO_9
H9WATER: neither is in LEVEL3O.SNI, so those arenas are silent in the original's
lookup too.

3. WHAT CHANGED
---------------
- formats::Sni::open: an archive by its DIRECTORY; entries are read from the
  file by range. Sni::parse (the whole file) is unchanged.
- audio::SoundBank::forLevel holds TRAVERSE.SNI and LEVELnS.SNI whole (the
  effects: 0.8-1.5 MB, any of which a frame may start with no warning) and
  OPENS LEVELnO.SNI. prefetch()/prefetchStep(): the original's stepped music
  read into one staging buffer, decoded on the last step; a get() of a sound
  whose prefetch is in flight finishes it and counts the stall.
- audio::LevelMusic: the port of src/Mdk.Game/Audio/LevelMusic.cs. The level's
  music was NOT PORTED before this: the tracks were resident and never played.
- Both frontends: music made with the runtime (its mixer), enter() + update()
  every step after the camera (C# Viewer.cs:521-525), the second arena's track
  prefetched when that arena changes, one step a frame. The 3DS bounds the
  decoded store at 6 MiB (LRU, never a playing voice) and, once a second, gives
  back every decoded track but the playing one, the fading one and the loading
  arena's (SoundBank::releaseMusicExcept): the spec's "free the decoded samples
  of an arena when it is deactivated".
- LevelData reads the corridors by entry from the opened archive: the level's
  load no longer reads 3.3-4.5 MB of music to get at 0.2-0.5 MB of corridors.
- An arena keeps its models section only up to its first embedded sound
  (2.73 MB of the six levels' 11.37 MB of models sections).
- formats::SpriteAnimation: the frame guard was "pixels <= the buffer's size",
  which an RLE frame held on its own breaks (K_SLIP is 7,931 bytes and its
  frames are larger); it is a fixed ceiling now.

Emulator timings are not the console's. THE LAST STEP IS THE ONE TO WATCH ON
HARDWARE: it decodes the whole track (half a million 8-bit samples to int16) in
one frame, 40-63 ms here. If the console shows it as a hitch, the fix is to
convert each step's bytes as they arrive instead of all at the end.

7. INDEPENDENT REVIEW (Fable subagent, code only, mutants run in its own copy)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
No finding in dangling pointers, LevelMusic's semantics against the C#, lookup
order on retail data, or retry storms. Fixed from it:
- HIGH: a voice's pin was released only by SoundMixer::forget(). The runtime
  owns its mixer and the frontend's bank outlives it, so every death left one
  pin per live voice -- with music playing, a megabyte a life that neither the
  budget nor releaseMusicExcept() could ever free (measured by the reviewer:
  4.78 MB unreleasable after seven lives). ~SoundMixer releases them now.
- Sni::open took "the 4-byte kind could not be read" for "not a sound": the
  level's load would then parse a music track as a corridor ('RIFF' is a
  material count of 1.18e9: an abort on the console). The open fails now, and
  Arena::parseWorldSection checks every count against the bytes that are left.
- one failed read made a track "missing" until the game was restarted: a READ
  failure is now retried the next time the name is asked for, at most three
  times; "not in the archives" and "not a WAV" are remembered as before.
- blocking audio work was not all timed or logged: every prefetch step is timed
  (worst read step and the decoding last step are on the line), and any frame
  in which the bank read a streamed sound whole says so.
- a streamed entry's length was never checked: 8 MiB ceiling, in the archive
  and in the bank before the staging buffer is reserved; stored offsets that
  would overflow are refused.
- a corridor that cannot be read is counted and said (LevelData::
  corridorReadFailures); the loading arena's track is asked for again once a
  second in case the budget evicted it.
MUTANTS, counted as run (each in a clean scratch copy of mdk_core + tests):
- 5 of the corridor read and the sound cut (corridor parsed 4 bytes in; cut at
  the last sound; cut 4096 and 64 bytes early; no cut): 3 caught at once, the
  4096-early and the no-cut ones survived until the kept span was pinned to the
  byte (keepAnimationBytes falls back to the WHOLE block when a track starts
  outside its span, which kept every bake right and hid the cut);
- 34 of the audio code and the parser's bounds -- the reviewer's survivors that
  are not equivalent, plus one or two per fix above: 24 caught on the first run,
  10 survived (guards whose removal changes no RESULT, only what is read or
  allocated first) and were caught after the tests were made to count the reads
  and the largest read asked for, and to feed each world-section count an absurd
  value. Two of those are caught by the test binary dying on a gigabyte
  allocation, which is the defect the guard is there against.
All 39 are caught now. NOT pinned by any test, said plainly: the sprite frame
ceiling removed altogether (only its value is pinned); the Linux frontend's
music wiring (the source check reads the 3DS file); playMusic's bus (existing
code, not this change's).
