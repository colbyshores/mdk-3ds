WHAT THE ORACLE SAYS, AND WHY THE TWO ARMS ARE NOT ONE ARM. `CheckTriggers` reads
the record's id as an index into the ARENA list (:726) and then splits on the type:
a SHOW box calls `ShowArena(arena)`, which takes the second slot ACTIVE, draws it,
starts its arena state and spawns its DTI aliens; a LOAD box calls
`PreloadArena(arena)`, which takes the same slot INACTIVE and does none of that.
A LOAD that names nothing goes through `ShowArena("")` instead of through the
preload (:731-734) — and `ShowArena("")` is the only way the second slot is ever
emptied from inside the trigger loop. Batch 139 walked CDANT_1, whose two boxes are
both type 3, so neither of those two arms ran. A port that swapped the arms, or
that dropped the empty-name case, passed every gate in the tree.
