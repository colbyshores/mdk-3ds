       review-8 F1, confirmed: this row claimed the corrected comment predicted
       32768/160 = 204.8/s and that the measurement was 0.02% off, and the
       prediction was wrong. ndsp.h:9 is `NDSP_SAMPLE_RATE (SYSCLOCK_SOC /
       512.0)` and os.h:9 is `SYSCLOCK_SOC (16756991u)`, so the DSP rate is
       32728.5 Hz and the prediction for a 160-sample frame is 204.6/s -- the
       measurement is 0.1% FAST for it, and secs is printed to 2 dp, so the
       measurement is 204.75 +/- 0.05 and the gap is real, not rounding. No

       and NOT the console; the Old-3DS mixer rate is unmeasured and the frame
       quantum is an assumption no installed header names.

       per ndspThreadMain iteration, after the svcSignalEvent that kicks the
       DSP -- and the old claim ("240-sample blocks at 48 kHz") fitted the same
       measurement at 200/s, which is why it survived three batches: a unit that
       only has to be right in magnitude cannot be falsified by a magnitude, and
       the first correction to it made the same mistake in the other direction.
