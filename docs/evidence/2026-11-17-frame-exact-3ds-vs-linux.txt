2026-11-17  Frame-exact 3DS vs Linux, and why they differ: the aspect ratio
==========================================================================

TOOL. `make AUTOQUIT=90 FIXED=1` gives every 3DS frame exactly one 1/60 s step
(the Linux --fixed-step). mdk_3ds/tools/compare_linux.sh <level> <frame> builds
that with CAPTURE=<frame> LEVEL=<level>, runs it in Mandarine (:99), pulls the
game's own left-eye readback from the lane's SD card, captures Linux
(--headless --fixed-step --size=400x240 --capture-frame=<frame>, frames +-1),
and prints the difference plus a Linux | 3DS | diff side-by-side. EMULATOR.

LEVEL 7, FRAME 300 (3DS 300 vs Linux 299, the closest; Linux frames 286-314 are
identical, a static view): 22.38% of pixels differ by >16, mean |d| 12.15.
71.4% of pixels are BIT-IDENTICAL, and the tone curve is the identity (3DS R
16/96/255 -> Linux 16/97/255), so palettes, expansion and texture data agree.
The difference survives a 4-pixel box blur (22.18%), so it is not texel
jitter: it is a displacement that grows the nearer a feature is to the camera.

CAUSE. mdk_linux/src/main.cpp fixes kAspect = 1280/960 (4:3, the original
game's) at every output size, so a 400x240 Linux capture is a 4:3 projection
STRETCHED to 5:3. The 3DS uses its panel's true 400/240. Both are the same core
FollowCamera; the aspect is an input to its projection and its placement.

PROOF. A temporary 3DS build with kAspect = 1280/960 (reverted, never
committed): 1.41% of pixels differ by >16, mean |d| 1.04 -- the same order as
the Linux build's own measured gap to the C# oracle (1.240%). Everything else
in the 3DS pipeline matches.
  docs/evidence/2026-11-17-compare-L7-f300-5x3.png           (as shipped, 5:3)
  docs/evidence/2026-11-17-compare-L7-f300-4x3-experiment.png (4:3, the proof)

OPEN, AN OWNER DECISION: which aspect the 3DS should show -- the true 5:3 view
(wider than the original's field of view, undistorted), the original's 4:3
stretched to fill 400x240 (what Linux shows at that size), or 4:3 pillarboxed
in 320x240 (the original's view, undistorted, with side bars).

OWNER DECISION (2026-11-17): KEEP THE TRUE 5:3. The 3DS keeps
kAspect = 400/240. The Linux frontend gains `--true-aspect` (project at the
output size's own ratio; off by default, so the Linux game keeps the original's
4:3), and compare_linux.sh passes it, so the comparison is between the same
framing. Level 7 frame 300, shipped 3DS (5:3) vs Linux --true-aspect: 1.33%
of pixels differ by >16, mean |d| 0.99.

THE CANVAS SAMPLED ITS PADDING (2026-11-17, same session). EMULATOR evidence:
Mandarine r1.2.1 (md5 0d2adab6ad078c78e6d03e72086f2eb6) on DISPLAY=:99, via
`mdk_3ds/tools/compare_linux.sh <level> 300` (3DS: make AUTOQUIT=90 FIXED=1
CAPTURE=300 LEVEL=<level>, left-eye readback; Linux: --headless --fixed-step
--true-aspect --size=400x240). Not a hardware result.

SYMPTOM. Across levels 3-8 the 3DS differed by 1.3-4.4%. The level 6 HUD read
"1nn" for "100" (docs/evidence/2026-11-17-hud-digits-L6-before.png, Linux left,
3DS right): the HUD region differed on 26.10% of pixels.

RULED OUT, MEASURED. Not placement: the green ring's bbox -- by a LOOSE green
test (G>150, R<100, B<100), not the HUD acceptance's exact (0,255,0) -- is
(335..386, 193..231/232) in both frames, and a cross-correlation of the HUD crop finds its
best match at shift (0,0). Not a half-pixel raster convention: half-pixel
nudges of the canvas projection gave 22.75-27.56% (HUD), and half-pixel UV
offsets per quad gave 24.94-27.38%.

CAUSE. 06_draw.inc applied the PICA's last-memory-row t rule
(pica::sceneUvFromTop) to every pass EXCEPT Overlay, on the argument that the
canvas projection's y inversion cancels it. It does not: the projection places
the quad, the uv picks the texel. A canvas image that is not a power of two
tall therefore read its transparent padding rows (the digits), and a
power-of-two one was upside down within its quad.

FIX. Every draw takes the rule. Level 6 HUD region: 26.10% -> 0.10%. The
frame, 3DS frame 300 vs the closest Linux frame (% of px differing by >16):

The residue on level 6 is the sky dome's ceiling (the panorama interpolated
across ~32x30-texel cells, sky_dome.h) and Kurt's silhouette edges.

THE HUD ACCEPTANCE HAD BEEN CALIBRATED ON THE FLIPPED PANEL. emurun --expect
hud's reach arm measured the (0,255,0) ink against the panel's derived far
corner (390,234) with a 2 px shortfall taken from the flipped image. Sampled
correctly, the ink's far corner is (384,218) in BOTH the Linux frame and the
3DS frame (192 pixels each, identical bbox), so the oracle itself would fail
it. The sprite's green-free border is now a measured constant
(HUD_INK_INSET_X/Y = 4/14, so 6 = 4 + 2 and 16 = 14 + 2); the 12 px budget, the
2 px calibration and the 20 px ceiling are unchanged, and
tests/scripts/emurun_harness_test.sh anchors its reach fixtures to the inset
corner (321 checks pass). emurun --expect hud: rc 0, marker 121/121, HUD INK
192 of 192 inside, bbox 335,193-384,218, 0 outside.

THE INK CORNER IS THE PANEL SPRITE'S, NOT THE DIGITS'. Exact (0,255,0) in both
frames after the fix: x=384 only at rows 203-205 and y=218 only at x 336-337,
the ring's own right and bottom edges, so the far corner does not move with
the health value shown. (The loose-green bbox above includes the ring's
darker greens, which reach y=231; the exact colour stops at 218.)
