2026-11-19  3DS edge smoothing 2x1 by default (2x2 behind edgeSmoothing2x2)
==========================================================================

HARDWARE. Old 3DS appliance old3ds-lan167 (CFGU model 0, 268 MHz, L2 none),
stereo ON, level 8, every enhancement on (the card has no config.ini), the
single-pass sun shadows of 20f337c. hw_cycle.sh FRESH on every run.

WHY. The per-toggle profile (2026-11-18-3ds-enhanced-cost-per-toggle.txt):
edge smoothing at 2x2 (480x800 eye targets the display transfer averages
2x2) cost ~22 ms of the Old 3DS's 80 ms stereo frame, and its 4.5 MB of eye
targets left the sun's map at 256 and the glow 714 KB of VRAM. The vault's
3ds-hardware-aa-via-display-transfer-downscale.md names 2x1 (SCALE_X: the
framebuffer's 240-wide axis doubled, 480x400) the pragmatic default, 2x2 only
with fill headroom; its many-pass note's "supersampling is nearly free" was a
New 3DS measurement and does not hold on this console.

WHAT. buildEyeTargets(r, sx, sy) and transferScaling(sx, sy): 1x1, 2x1
(SCALE_X) or 2x2 (SCALE_XY); targetScaleX/Y beside targetScale (the larger).
applyEdgeSmoothing: 2x1 with enableEdgeSmoothing, 2x2 when config.ini's
edgeSmoothing2x2 = 1 (the PC ignores it). The glow's shrink and the capture
readback take the same scaling as the display. The right eye still borrows
the left eye's depth whenever the targets are scaled.

MEASURED (hw_cycle --profile; p50 is the 0.1 ms bucket's upper edge):
  2x2 (20f337c)    p50 63.7 ms  mean 77.1 ms  begin 21.0  draw 15.7  end 19.6
  2x1              p50 53.8 ms  mean 67.2 ms  begin 14.1  draw 14.7  end 19.3
  -> ~10 ms a frame; ~18.6 fps against 12.5 at the start of 2026-11-18's
  measurements (80.3 ms).
VRAM after the eye targets: 2x2 1,683,456 free (the map 256); 2x1 3,987,456
free -- the sun's map is 512 again ("shadows: map 512x512 (VRAM, 2938880
free)") and the glow keeps 2.2 MB.

THE LOOK (hw_cycle --capture, CAPTURE=60 FIXED=1, the console's left eye):
  docs/evidence/2026-11-19-3ds-edge-smoothing-2x1-vs-2x2-console.png
  docs/evidence/2026-11-19-3ds-edge-smoothing-2x1-vs-2x2-console-zoom.png
At the panel's size the two read the same; zoomed, 2x1 steps a little more on
near-horizontal edges (it averages one axis only) and its shadow edges are
crisper (the map is 512, not 256). The owner judges on the console:
`edgeSmoothing2x2=1` in sdmc:/3ds/mdk/config.ini brings 2x2 back.

TESTS. tests/formats/lighting_tests.cpp: edgeSmoothing2x2 off by default,
written, read back. The c3d_frame pins hold (the glow's shrink is still
tiled -> linear with scaling, no bit 5).

REVIEW (independent, read-only, Fable): no blocker; transfer dims, bit 5,
viewport/scissor, the canvas mapping, the glow's scratch and copy, the depth
borrow, runtime toggling and the config checked. Fixed:
- a refused 2x2 fell straight to 1x1 and stayed there: it now steps down to
  2x1 first, each refusal latched until the setting that asked for it goes off;
- the capture readback's scale names shadowed its loop's sx/sy: renamed;
- the glow's shrink re-derived the scaling flag: it calls transferScaling;
- stale "scale-2" log text.
Recorded, not done: at 2x1 the right eye could own its depth (four 768 KB
buffers fit) and skip a split and a depth fill per stereo frame -- an A/B
for later, against the map's and the glow's VRAM; there is no in-game menu
row for 2x2 (config.ini only, like shadowMapSize); the glow's two 768 KB
scratch buffers stay until exit when smoothing goes off (older than this).
