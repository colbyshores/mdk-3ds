what this pair holds, unchanged from the pair it supersedes: review-4 F6 charged
that `BlendState::enable` was never read by the renderer -- a struct that could
say "disabled" and blend anyway. Batch 85 makes the field load-bearing
(`installBlendState` installs `mdk::pica::kBlendReplace` whenever `enable` is
false). The claim being tested is that this is a representation fix and not a
picture fix, and the pair says so:

    panel crop, parent vs this build:    0 px of 96000
    whole capture, parent vs this build: 180 px, all of them at
                                         x 743..767, y 744..752

Those 180 pixels are Mandarine drawing its own `Frame: N.NN ms` overlay on the
BOTTOM screen -- the documented noise floor for two runs of any two builds (64
-112 px in that band, and the two runs ended 60 frames apart, so the digits
differ). The panel crop exists precisely so that overlay is not compared.
Neither half was cropped to make the diff small: the crop is the top screen,
the diff is the bottom one, and both numbers are recorded.

And what this pair CANNOT say, stated rather than hidden: this scene is 154
all-Solid batches, so neither half exercises a blended pass. The register state
for `Pass::Blended` and `Pass::Overlay` is held by `PASS PicaMatrix` and the
frame gate, not by either picture. Review-5 reached the same conclusion by a
shorter route: for every `(pass, blend)` the shipped `blendStateForPass` returns
either `kBlendReplace` with `enable == false` or a state with `enable == true`,
so `s.enable ? s : kBlendReplace` is `s` for every input and the four
`C3D_AlphaBlend` arguments are unchanged by construction. The 0 px is therefore
guaranteed by the code and does not depend on this capture at all.
