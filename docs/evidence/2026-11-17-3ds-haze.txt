WHAT THE PC DOES. enhanced.frag:187: fog = 1 - exp(-density * |position - eye|),
mixed toward the level's sky-bottom palette colour, on the lit and sprite modes
only (the sky shader and the canvas take none). density = 0.0002
(level_light.cpp kHazeDensity).

WHY NOT THE PICA FOG UNIT. It indexes a 128-entry table by the STORED depth. With
this camera (near 0.5, far 20000) and the pinned z-buffer depth map, stored depth
passes 0.99 by ~64 units, so the whole visible world sits between the table's
first two entries: at 1000 units the table would keep 0.08 of the colour where
the PC keeps exp(-0.2) = 0.82. A w-buffer fixes the index but replaces the depth
map of the original look for a cosmetic toggle, and coarsens precision near the
eye where the depth pull separates posters by tiny fractions.

WHAT THE 3DS DOES. All on the PICA200 (SPEC.md 7.9: no CPU fallback):
  - mdk_c3d.v.pica: uniform `haze` (x = -density*log2(e)); each vertex keeps
    ex2(x * clip.w) of its colour (clip.w = eye depth) in the primary alpha.
  - combiner stage 1: INTERPOLATE(previous, constant = haze colour, primary
    alpha), installed only for draws pica::hazeApplies (Lit/Sprite shading, not
    Overlay, toggle on, rig live); a passthrough, uniform 0, otherwise.
  - The CPU: the light rig is rebuilt when the ARENA changes, not per frame
    (enhancedLighting walks the sky panorama and every arena triangle), and one
    uniform write per haze on/off transition.
  Approximations against the PC: per-vertex not per-pixel, eye depth not
  Euclidean distance, the mix in sRGB not linear.
  Host-pinned: tests/scripts/pica_matrix_tests.cpp
  testTheHazeIsThePcCurveOnTheRightDraws (the curve within 1e-5 of the PC's to
  20000 units; which draws take it; the colour word).
