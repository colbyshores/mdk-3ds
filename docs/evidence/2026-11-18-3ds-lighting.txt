3DS ENHANCED MODE: LIGHTING ON THE PICA200 (SPEC.md 7.9, a ninth toggle enableLighting,
menu row LIGHTING), 2026-11-18. EMULATOR evidence -- Mandarine r1.2.1 (md5
0d2adab6ad078c78e6d03e72086f2eb6) on DISPLAY=:99 via compare_linux.sh (make
AUTOQUIT=90 FIXED=1 CAPTURE=300 LEVEL=<n>). Not a hardware result: the geometry
shader's Old-3DS cost is owed to the hardware pass.

THE PC. enhanced.frag mode 1: a Lit surface's FLAT normal from the world
position's screen derivatives, turned to the camera; light = mix(ground, sky,
n.z * 0.5 + 0.5) + sun * max(n . -sunDir, 0) (+ point lights, x shadow); lit =
to_srgb(tonemap(to_linear(albedo) * exposure * light, 0.6)).

THE 3DS. A second shader program: the same vertex shader (now also writing the
world position from a per-draw world matrix) plus a GEOMETRY SHADER
(mdk_c3d.g.pica), bound for Lit triangle draws while the toggle and the rig are
live. It sees the whole triangle, so its face normal (cross of two edges) IS the
flat normal; the hemisphere and the sun are directional, so one evaluation per
triangle IS the per-pixel value. It emits F = to_srgb(exposure * light) / 2 per
channel in the vertex colour; combiner stage 1 multiplies the texture by it,
scaled x2 (haze moved to stage 2). Below the PC's knee this is exact:
to_srgb(to_linear(a) * k) = a * to_srgb(k) for GAMMA a pure power. Above it the
PC's soft shoulder becomes a clamp. Not here: point lights, the sun's shadow,
ambient occlusion, the post pass. Sprites, the sky, the canvas and Lines meshes
are not lit (the PC's modes 2-4 are unlit too).

FOUND AND FIXED ON THE WAY (each measured in the emulator):
  - picasso: one input register per instruction, at most one uniform, uniforms
    as the first source, r0-r15 only.
  - The knee is the PC's own constant 0.6 (Tonemap.Knee, 10_enhanced.inc), not
    the rig's sky alpha (1.0). A first version tone-mapped exposure * light
    alone, which saturated every face of an indoor arena (level 6: exposure
    1.34 against a sky of ~1) and lit nothing; the tone curve is now the clamp.
  - float24 stops near 2^-62: a 1e-20 degenerate-normal floor is 0; 1e-12.
  - THE GEOMETRY SHADER'S UNIFORMS MUST BE WRITTEN AT EVERY BIND OF ITS PROGRAM.
    Written once per frame, every lit draw that followed an unlit one (an object
    after the sky or a sprite) read ZEROS: a lamp's wire frame lit (1.43, 0, 0)
    -- one channel right, two zero -- and black when the light was set to the
    sky colour alone, while the level geometry drawn before any switch was
    right. With the six uniforms re-written on each bind it lights normally
    ((70,65,56) -> (68,64,54), a face turned down). Bisected with a constant F
    (lit path pixel-identical to unlit: 0 px differ), the pre-sRGB light, and the
    sky-only light.

REVIEW (independent; citro3d's libcitro3d.a disassembled) AND WHAT CHANGED.
  - THE ZERO-UNIFORM CAUSE, now recorded in code: C3D_BindProgram of a program
    without a geometry shader calls C3Di_ClearShaderUniforms(GPU_GEOMETRY_SHADER)
    (on the PICA200 the geometry unit runs vertex shading when no GS is bound).
    Writing them at every lit bind is the fix; that this matches the CONSOLE (not
    only Mandarine) is owed to the hardware pass.
  - The toggle: the PC's enableFragmentLights switches only its point lights, and
    the menu is shared, so the 3DS light has its own ninth toggle, enableLighting
    (menu row LIGHTING); enableFragmentLights keeps its label and is reserved for
    3DS point lights.
  - The world output is semantic `dummy`: the geometry shader receives whole
    registers, and the rasteriser no longer interpolates a field nothing reads
    (the original look's vertices still run the ~7 extra VS instructions --
    an Old-3DS cost to measure).
  - The identity world matrix is written only when it is not already there;
    program switches per frame are logged (`light: frame N: K program
    switches`), the number a sort-by-program would be judged by.
  - Precision of the claims: exact while albedo_lin * exposure * light < 0.6 PER
    CHANNEL; above it the clamp is brighter than the PC's shoulder (at a linear
    product of 1.0: 1.0 against the PC's 0.930 sRGB, ~7%). The x2 scale halves
    the 8-bit light's precision (steps of 2/255). Lit Lines meshes (the PC lights
    them as lines) stay unlit here: this GPU draws no lines.
