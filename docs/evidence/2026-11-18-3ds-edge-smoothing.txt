3DS Enhanced Mode edge smoothing (enhancement 4 of 7, SPEC.md 7.9) -- EMULATOR evidence
=======================================================================================

Instrument: Mandarine r1.2.1 (20250128-418c25c, md5 0d2adab6...), via
mdk_3ds/tools/compare_linux.sh -> emurun.sh, DISPLAY=:99, forced llvmpipe OpenGL.
Builds: make -C mdk_3ds AUTOQUIT=90 FIXED=1 CAPTURE=300 LEVEL=3 (compare_linux.sh).
Scene: level 3 start, frame 300, fixed 1/60 s clock. NOT a hardware result.

WHAT IT IS
  Toggle enableEdgeSmoothing (config.ini key, 10th row "EDGE SMOOTHING" in the
  SELECT menu). On: both eye targets are 480x800 (2x per axis) and the display
  transfer that already copies each target to the panel averages every 2x2
  block (GX_TRANSFER_SCALE_XY). Supersampling by the PICA's own transfer
  engine, the counterpart of the PC's MSAA; no CPU work, no extra pass.

  VRAM is 6 MB and a 2x eye is 1.5 MB colour + 1.5 MB depth, so the right eye
  is created without depth and BORROWS the left eye's buffer (4.5 MB in all).
  installEye splits the frame (C3D_FrameSplit: the left eye's commands go to the
  GX queue) and then clears the depth before the right eye draws. The targets
  are rebuilt between frames when the toggle changes (C3D_RenderTargetDelete
  waits on the GPU queue); a failed 2x build falls back to 1x with one
  "smoothing: ... REFUSED" line and is not retried until the toggle goes off.

  Guest log (L3): "targets: 240x400 per eye, scale 1 (vram free 4755456)" at
  create, then "targets: 480x800 per eye, scale 2, right eye borrows the left
  depth (vram free 1683456)" at the first frame.

RESULTS
  Mono, smoothing only (all other toggles off), vs Linux original look:
    9.44% px differ by >16 (the original-look baseline at this scene is 2.14%).
    The difference image is edges only: the original look is nearest-sampled
    and aliased, and smoothing averages every edge.
    2026-11-18-3ds-edge-smoothing-zoom.png: Linux (left) vs 3DS smoothed
    (right), 4x crop of a building edge against the sky.
  Stereo (MDK_EMU_STEREO=60: render_3d=1, factor_3d=60), smoothing only:
    both eyes captured (writeScreenshotBmp now writes <path>.right.bmp when two
    passes ran); both whole, with parallax.
    2026-11-18-3ds-edge-smoothing-eyes.png: left | right.
  NON-VACUITY: the same stereo run with the split+clear removed: the right eye
    differs from the correct one in 43.4% of pixels (the left eye's depth rejects
    it); the left eye 0.008%. 2026-11-18-3ds-edge-smoothing-noclear-right.png.
    The ordering is pinned in tests/scripts/c3d_frame_test.sh
    (borrowed-depth-split-then-cleared-per-right-eye, two arms: clear removed,
    clear before the split).

  Readback defect found on the way: a scaled GX_DisplayTransfer takes the
  INPUT-sized dimension for BOTH buffers (citro3d's own output path does the
  same); passing the panel's 240x400 as the output read a quarter of the
  target, unscaled, twice. Fixed in writeScreenshotBmp.

NOT VERIFIED (hardware pass, at the end)
  - Fill-rate cost: 4x the pixels per eye. The owner A/Bs the toggle's cost on
    the Old 3DS; nothing here measures it.
  - The borrowed depth + FrameSplit ordering on real GX hardware (the emulator
    honoured it; the right eye broke without it, so the emulator models it).
  - VRAM headroom on hardware at scale 2 (1.68 MB free in the emulator).

INDEPENDENT REVIEW (2026-11-18), and what changed
  - Capture race (medium, hardware-only): the readback was a bare
    GX_DisplayTransfer + PPF wait after C3D_FrameEnd, which the queue's own
    panel transfer could satisfy first. Now C3D_SyncDisplayTransfer (drains the
    queue, runs this transfer, waits for it).
  - The right capture is named hwcap.right.bmp.log, not hwcap.bmp.log.right.bmp:
    the console's HTTP serves only /3ds/**.log and .txt.
  - At scale 2 the per-frame right-eye clear is colour-only: its depth is the
    left eye's buffer, already filled, and filled again before the right eye.
  - Checked correct by the reviewer: borrowed-depth ownership (no double free),
    target deletion only between frames, the readback dimensions, toggles set
    before the first begin_frame, failure paths, the emurun change under
    set -euo pipefail.
  - Not changed: the census line still reports extents in panel pixels (it reads
    NDC; a diagnostic only).
  After the fixes the stereo run's two eyes are re-measured against the run
  above (see the commit's verification).
