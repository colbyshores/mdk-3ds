WHAT THE PC DOES. The enhanced look samples the world's colour textures
trilinear + 16x anisotropic, filters the sky panorama (Panorama::linear) and
filters the canvas by hand (palette_filtered); a filtered texel is cut at
HALF_COVER (alpha < 0.5 discarded) with premultiplied colour divided back.

WHAT THE 3DS DOES. All on the PICA200:
  - every draw's texture is bound GPU_LINEAR while enableFiltering is on,
    GPU_NEAREST otherwise (06_draw.inc; a bit in the texture's parameter word,
    so the toggle is live with no reload);
  - the alpha test's reference is pica::alphaTestReference(filtering): 0 (the
    oracle's discard) off, 127 (the PC's half cover, alpha >= 0.5 kept) on;
  - at texture creation (cold) pica::prepareForFiltering gives each alpha-0
    texel the mean colour of its opaque 8-neighbours (alpha stays 0), so a
    filtered cut-out edge is not pulled toward black -- the PICA's combiner
    cannot divide by alpha as the PC does -- and copies the last real row and
    column one texel into the padding of a non-power-of-two axis, the PC's
    clamp, so an opaque edge does not fade into a crack under the half cover.
  Not matched: anisotropy (the PICA has none) and mipmaps (the next step, a
  memory cost to measure first); the canvas takes the half-cover cut where the
  PC blends canvas edges by cover.
  Host-pinned: pica_matrix_tests testTheFilteredEdge (the bleed's exact means,
  alpha untouched, opaque texels untouched, the clamp, a power-of-two no-op;
  the reference values). Frame gate: c3d_frame_test's alpha-test pin now reads
  alphaTestReference(r->toggles.enableFiltering), with a new arm
  (alpha-test-ignores-the-filtering-toggle) that goes red if the toggle is
  dropped. Config/menu: tests/formats EnhancedConfigRoundTrips and
  EnhancedMenuTogglesInPlace cover the sixth toggle and row.

REVIEW (independent, same day) AND WHAT CHANGED.
  F1 HIGH -- the half-cover cut deleted translucent CANVAS quads with filtering
     on: the hurt flash (alpha 0.4) never drew, the white flash and the
     inventory box (0.5) were cut, the skull popped in at half fade. The PC's
     enhanced.frag returns for its canvas modes BEFORE the HALF_COVER discard.
     FIX: the reference is per draw, pica::alphaTestReferenceFor(filtering,
     pass) -- half cover for a filtered scene draw, 0 for Overlay and for
     nearest -- written by ONE cached helper, installAlphaTest, which installEye
     calls forced and drawRecorded calls per draw. c3d_frame_test's pins follow
     (one C3D_AlphaTest site, inside installAlphaTest; installEye's forced
     call; drawRecorded's per-pass call), with two new arms (the pass ignored;
     installEye's call removed). PicaMatrix pins the values, including that a
     0.4-alpha canvas fragment (102) passes with filtering on.
  F2 MEDIUM -- animated textures stack their frames vertically in one texture,
     so a bilinear tap at a frame edge took the neighbouring frame's row.
     FIX: a draw with frame_count > 1 stays NEAREST (the PC samples one array
     layer and cannot bleed; this is the honest subset).
  F3 LOW-MEDIUM -- a sky slice 1024 wide REPEATs onto its own far column, a
     one-texel seam at each slice boundary and the panorama's wrap. FIX:
     sky_dome slices store one neighbour column on each side (wrapped), drawn
     columns <= 1022; uv runs over the stored width. Host-pinned (SkyDome: the
     stored edge columns are the panorama's wrapped neighbours; every band
     vertex still on its oracle column, worst 0.0004).
  F4/F5 -- prepareForFiltering also runs when an EVICTED texture is made again
     mid-frame, so its per-texel divide became an exact reciprocal table
     (verified exhaustively for every sum 0..2040 and n 1..8) and the R11 pin

  Tests: testTheFilteredEdge now uses a legal 8x8 tile, adds a one-padded-axis
