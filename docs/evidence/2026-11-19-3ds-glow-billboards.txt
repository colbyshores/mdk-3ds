2026-11-19  3DS glow as additive billboards by default (the ported bloom behind glowBloom)
=====================================================================================

HARDWARE + HOST. Old 3DS appliance old3ds-lan167 (CFGU model 0, 268 MHz, L2
none), stereo ON, level 8, every enhancement on, edge smoothing 2x1 (7e46bf6),
hw_cycle.sh FRESH on every run. The Linux build for the PC's look.

THE DECISION (owner, 2026-11-18): the PC keeps its bloom (post.frag: the frame's
blurred mips 3 and 5, everything past 0.8 linear, at 0.8); the 3DS draws
additive billboards on bright effects instead -- "the screen is so small that
the player can't make out that they are billboards" (the owner's experience
from ../t2k) -- with the bloom kept as an option to compare.

WHY. The bloom cost ~10 ms of the Old 3DS's stereo frame (the per-toggle
profile) for a look the PC itself barely shows in ordinary play: the Linux
build, glow on against off, frame 60 -- level 8: 0 pixels brighter by >8;
level 7: 169 (one faint line along the floor's highlight); level 3: 0.

THE BILLBOARDS. Not invented: the vault's tsunami2010 soft-dot recipe
(30-architecture/patterns/3ds-citra-mandarine-no-geometry-shader-use-cpu-quads.md:
CPU-built quads, a radial-gradient texture, TEXTURE0 x PRIMARY, additive
SRC_ALPHA/ONE), measured there on hardware.
- WHAT GLOWS is what the PC's bloom would catch: a sprite effect whose texture
  has texels past the bloom's threshold in linear colour (effectsGlowOf, once
  per texture -- again when a streamed texture's pixels arrive; the share of
  opaque texels that pass and their mean colour).
- EACH glowing effect hands over one halo (render.h mdk_render_set_glow_quads;
  render_types.h GlowQuad): its own billboard quad, twice the size, its bright
  colour at the bloom's 0.8 gain times the share. The PC ignores them.
- THE 3DS draws them where the bloom ran (drawGlow -> drawGlowBillboards, once
  per eye before the canvas): one linear vertex buffer written once a frame, a
  32x32 soft dot, depth-tested and not written, additive, one draw per halo
  with its colour as the tint. No target switch, no transfer, no split.
- config.ini glowBloom = 1 brings the ported bloom back (the PC ignores it).

MEASURED (hw_cycle --profile, level 8):
  glow as the bloom (glowBloom=1)   p50 53.5 ms  mean 66.9 ms
  glow as billboards (default)      p50 49.9 ms  mean 61.4 ms
  -> ~3.6 ms (p50), ~5.5 ms (mean). The frame across today's branch: 80.3 ->
  49.9 ms p50 (12.5 -> ~20 fps).
THE PATH ON HARDWARE (make ... GLOWPROBE=1: one warm halo, 30 units ahead of
the camera, 12 across): drawn, additive, soft, occluded by Kurt in front of it;
"1 halos now (2 drawn, both eyes)".
  docs/evidence/2026-11-19-3ds-glow-billboard-probe-console.png

NOT VERIFIED. A REAL effect's halo: level 8's opening has NO sprite effects at
all ("effects: frame N: 0 sprite effects" every 300 frames), and the port does
not yet fire Kurt's gun (no code reads the fire key), so no run without hands
makes muzzle flashes or impacts. Which of the game's effect textures pass the
threshold, and how their halos read against the PC's bloom on the same frame,
waits for a scene where the level scripts spawn effects.

TESTS. tests/scripts/vm_tests.cpp: effectsGlowOf (white and a 0.95 red pass,
mid grey does not, transparent texels are not counted, no pixels / nothing
opaque -> none). tests/formats/lighting_tests.cpp: glowBloom off by default,
written, read back. c3d_frame_test.sh: drawGlowBillboards among the draw and
program-bind sites.

REVIEW (independent, read-only, Fable): no blocker; the mesh write's timing,
the state left for the canvas, the geometry-stage rule, the uv, stereo, the
std::map reference, teardown and Linux checked. Fixed:
- HIGH: the "pixels back?" test called Texture::indices(), which LOADS pixels
  that are away -- a mid-frame re-parse, repeated per frame on a failure. It
  reads pixelsAway() / residentBytes() now and never loads.
- HIGH: the halo shared its sprite's plane, and the sprite writes depth: on
  the console's 24-bit floats a LEQUAL halo could round behind it and cut a
  hole where it is brightest. It sits half the sprite's size towards the
  camera now.
- the halo added the bright texels' whole colour; the PC's bloom adds only
  max(linear - 0.8, 0) at 0.8 (at most 0.16 linear). The measure is that excess
  now, and the halo adds to_srgb(0.8 * excess) times the bright share.
- a frame with no runtime redrew the last frame's halos: they are cleared at
  the end of every frame.
- the 64-halo cap was silent: the log counts what it drops.
Recorded, not done: halos are unhazed and can add over glass or OnTop draws in
front of them (they come after those passes); glowBloom with the bloom
refused leaves no glow (no fall back to billboards); the bloom's VRAM is not
freed when switching to billboards at runtime; the counters count instances.
